# Account Documents User Guide

This guide will help you understand how to use the Account Documents feature to generate and download official documents for your account.

## 📋 Overview

The Account Documents feature allows you to generate three types of official documents:

1. **Account Statement** - Your complete transaction history and balance details
2. **Proof of Account** - Official verification of your account status
3. **Account Agreement** - Terms and conditions you agreed to

All documents are generated in PDF format and can be downloaded instantly.

## 🚀 How to Access Account Documents

### Step 1: Login to Your Account
1. Open the Mudrexx Earn application
2. Click on "Sign In" button
3. Enter your email and password
4. Click "Sign In" to access your account

### Step 2: Navigate to Profile Page
1. Once logged in, click on your profile icon or name
2. Select "Profile" from the dropdown menu
3. Or navigate directly to `/profile` in your browser

### Step 3: Find Account Documents Section
1. Scroll down on the Profile page
2. Look for the "Account Documents" section
3. You'll see three document cards:
   - Account Statement
   - Proof of Account
   - Account Agreement

## 📥 Downloading Documents

### Account Statement

**What it contains:**
- Your complete transaction history
- Balance breakdown (Real, Frozen, Demo)
- Frozen funds details
- Asset holdings summary
- Transaction statistics

**How to download:**
1. Find the "Account Statement" card
2. Click the "Download Statement" button
3. Wait for the PDF to generate (usually 2-3 seconds)
4. The PDF will automatically download to your computer
5. Check your Downloads folder for the file

**File name format:** `account-statement-{ID}.pdf`

### Proof of Account

**What it contains:**
- Official verification of your account
- Account holder details
- Account status (Active, Verified)
- Current balance snapshot
- Verification details
- Account age and activity

**How to download:**
1. Find the "Proof of Account" card
2. Click the "Download Proof" button
3. Wait for the PDF to generate
4. The PDF will automatically download
5. Check your Downloads folder

**File name format:** `proof-of-account-{ID}.pdf`

### Account Agreement

**What it contains:**
- Terms and conditions
- Trading risk disclosure
- Privacy policy summary
- Your acceptance record
- Signature lines

**How to download:**
1. Find the "Account Agreement" card
2. Click the "Download Agreement" button
3. Wait for the PDF to generate
4. The PDF will automatically download
5. Check your Downloads folder

**File name format:** `account-agreement-{ID}.pdf`

## 📄 Understanding Your Documents

### Account Statement

The Account Statement is divided into several sections:

1. **Account Holder Information**
   - Your name, email, phone
   - Registration date
   - Invite code

2. **Balance Summary**
   - Real Balance (INR and USDT)
   - Frozen Balance (funds in orders/staking)
   - Demo Balance (practice credits)
   - Total Converted (demo to real)

3. **Transaction Summary**
   - Total number of transactions
   - Breakdown by type (deposits, withdrawals, etc.)

4. **Recent Transactions**
   - Last 20 transactions with details
   - Transaction type, amount, status

5. **Frozen Funds**
   - Any funds currently locked
   - Reason for freezing
   - Status

### Proof of Account

The Proof of Account includes:

1. **Document Information**
   - Unique document ID
   - Issue date and validity period

2. **Account Holder Details**
   - Complete personal information
   - Registration details

3. **Account Status**
   - Active/Inactive status
   - Verification status
   - KYC status

4. **Current Balances**
   - Snapshot of all balances at generation time

5. **Verification Details**
   - Email verification status
   - Phone verification status
   - 2FA status
   - Account age

### Account Agreement

The Account Agreement contains:

1. **Agreement Information**
   - Agreement ID and version
   - Issue date

2. **Terms and Conditions**
   - Account Terms
   - Trading Risks
   - Demo Account rules
   - Fees and Charges
   - Privacy Policy

3. **User Acceptance**
   - Your acceptance record
   - Date and time of acceptance

4. **Signature Lines**
   - Space for your signature
   - Date field

## 💡 Tips and Best Practices

### When to Use Each Document

**Account Statement:**
- For personal record keeping
- When applying for loans or financial services
- For tax purposes
- To track your trading activity

**Proof of Account:**
- When verifying your account to third parties
- For KYC (Know Your Customer) requirements
- When applying for financial services
- As official account verification

**Account Agreement:**
- For your personal records
- When you need to review the terms
- For legal or compliance purposes
- To understand your rights and obligations

### Document Storage

1. **Save copies** - Keep copies of all downloaded documents
2. **Organize by date** - Create folders for different months/years
3. **Backup regularly** - Store backups in cloud storage
4. **Secure storage** - Keep documents in a secure location

### Document Validity

- **Account Statement**: Valid at the time of generation
- **Proof of Account**: Valid for 30 days from issue date
- **Account Agreement**: Valid as long as your account is active

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Problem: PDF not downloading**
- Solution: Check your browser's popup blocker
- Solution: Ensure JavaScript is enabled
- Solution: Try a different browser

**Problem: Document generation takes too long**
- Solution: Wait a few more seconds (large accounts may take longer)
- Solution: Check your internet connection
- Solution: Try again later

**Problem: Error message appears**
- Solution: Make sure you're logged in
- Solution: Check your internet connection
- Solution: Try refreshing the page

**Problem: Document shows incorrect information**
- Solution: Documents are generated in real-time
- Solution: If data is wrong, contact support
- Solution: Check if you're viewing the correct account

### Getting Help

If you encounter any issues:

1. **Check the browser console** - Press F12 and look for errors
2. **Contact support** - Use the Support page in the app
3. **Provide details** - Include the error message and what you were trying to do
